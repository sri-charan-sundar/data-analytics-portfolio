# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Experience Analysis — Python

## Objective

Analyze e-commerce transactions to identify insights around sales performance, delivery performance, customer satisfaction, discount effectiveness, and seasonality.

## Tools

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## Business Questions

- Which product categories and regions are driving revenue?
- Does delivery performance affect customer satisfaction?
- Are discounts being used effectively?
- What quarterly patterns are visible in revenue?

## Key Findings

- Electronics generated the highest revenue.
- West was the highest-revenue region.
- Delivery time showed only a weak relationship with customer ratings.
- Higher discount levels were associated with lower average revenue per order.
- Q2 generated the highest quarterly revenue.
