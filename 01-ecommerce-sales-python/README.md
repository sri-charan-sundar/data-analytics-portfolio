# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales & Customer Experience Analysis — Python

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce transaction data to understand sales performance, customer experience, delivery performance, discount effectiveness, and seasonal trends.

The analysis follows a business-focused approach to transform transaction-level data into actionable insights and recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective is to identify key factors influencing:

- Revenue performance
- Product and regional sales
- Delivery performance
- Customer satisfaction
- Discount effectiveness
- Seasonal sales patterns

---

## ❓ Business Questions

1. Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
2. Which regions contribute the most to sales?
3. Does delivery performance influence customer ratings?
4. Are higher discounts associated with higher revenue?
5. Which quarter generates the highest revenue?
6. What business actions can be recommended from the analysis?

## 📊 Key Findings

### Product Performance
Electronics generated the highest revenue among the analyzed product categories.

### Regional Performance
The West region generated the highest revenue.

### Delivery & Customer Satisfaction
Delivery time showed only a weak relationship with customer ratings, suggesting that delivery time alone may not strongly determine customer satisfaction.

### Discount Effectiveness
Higher discount levels were associated with lower average revenue per order, indicating that deeper discounts may not necessarily improve revenue efficiency.

### Seasonality
Q2 generated the highest quarterly revenue.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Investigate the factors driving strong Electronics performance and identify opportunities to scale successful products.
- Study the West region's sales drivers and evaluate whether successful strategies can be replicated in other regions.
- Avoid relying solely on delivery speed to improve customer satisfaction; investigate additional customer-experience factors.
- Evaluate discount strategies based on revenue efficiency rather than discount volume alone.
- Analyze seasonal demand patterns to improve inventory and promotional planning.
